🔄 <b>Flexible vs Locked: When to choose the type of earn product</b>

<b>📋 Step-by-step guide:</b>

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>1️⃣ Assess your liquidity needs</b>
💧 If you plan to withdraw funds frequently — choose <b>Flexible</b>: you can exit anytime without losing profit.
🔒 If you're ready to "freeze" the asset for a specific period — <b>Locked</b> will provide a higher rate.

<b>2️⃣ Compare yield (APY)</b>
• <b>Flexible:</b> 2–6% (daily accrual, reduced volatility risk).
• <b>Locked:</b> 6–20%+ (depending on term 7–180 days, network, and token type).
💡 <i>Example:</i> USDT Flexible on Binance — ~5% annually; BTC Locked on Bybit — ~7% for 60 days.

<b>3️⃣ When to choose Flexible</b>
• Unstable market or correction expected.
• Short-term holding of stablecoins (USDT, USDC).
• Need quick liquidity for new opportunities (IEO, airdrops).
🧭 <i>Strategy:</i> Keep 70% of deposit in Flexible and 30% in Locked — for balance between income and risk.

<b>4️⃣ When to choose Locked</b>
• Long-term HODL approach (BTC, ETH, TON).
• High confidence in the coin and desire to maximize profit.
• Using <b>Auto-Renew</b> for automatic reinvestment.
💰 <i>Example:</i> ETH Locked for 90 days via OKX Earn — ~9% APY with auto-renewal.

<b>5️⃣ Check early withdrawal terms</b>
⚙️ Flexible — instant access without penalties.
⏳ Locked — withdrawal after term completion; early withdrawal — with interest loss or impossible.
❗ Clarify the policy of the specific exchange — some platforms (Bybit, OKX) offer "Early Unlock" with partial penalty.

<b>6️⃣ Combined strategy (Pro approach 2025)</b>
🧩 Use <b>Dual Investment</b> or <b>Shark Fin</b> for hedging volatility.
📊 Distribute profit:
• 50% in Flexible (for maneuverability)
• 30% in Locked (for steady income)
• 20% in strategic Earn products with bonuses or cashback.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>✅ Flexible Advantages:</b>
• High liquidity
• Daily interest accrual
• Suitable for traders and active users

<b>🚀 Locked Advantages:</b>
• Higher APY (2–3 times)
• Fixed income regardless of volatility
• Discipline of long-term storage

<b>⚠️ Locked Risks:</b>
• Inability to quickly react to market
• Potential profit loss when coin price drops

<b>💡 Tip:</b>
• Start with <b>Flexible</b> for testing and analyzing daily APY.
• If yield is stable >5 days — gradually move part to <b>Locked</b>.
• Check current rates via bot or on exchange pages (Binance Earn, Bybit Savings, OKX Grow).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔎 <b>Quick reference:</b> 
<b>• Flexible</b> = control & freedom to use coins 💧 
<b>• Locked</b> = higher profit & longer coin holding 💰
