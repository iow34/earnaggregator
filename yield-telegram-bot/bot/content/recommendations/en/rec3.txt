📊 <b>Criteria for Choosing Earn Products for Investment</b>

<b>📋 Key Criteria:</b>

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>📘 Main Goal:</b>
To find a balance between profitability (APY), asset security, and flexibility of access to funds.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>1️⃣ Asset Type: Stability vs. Higher Yield Potential</b>

💵 <b>Stablecoins</b> (USDT, USDC, DAI):
• Low risk and predictable returns (2–6% APY).
• Ideal for beginners and short-term investments.

⚡ <b>Altcoins</b> (ETH, TON, SOL, AVAX):
• Higher earning potential (6–20% APY).
• But carry the risk of price drops during volatile markets.

💡 <i>Example:</i> USDT Flexible on Bybit — ~4% APY, low risk.
TON Locked for 60 days — ~10% APY, but with the risk of price fluctuation.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>2️⃣ APY vs. Risk</b>

📈 <b>APY > 10%</b> — usually comes with increased risk (altcoins, DeFi platforms).
🛡️ <b>APY < 5%</b> — more stable programs (Binance, OKX, Bybit Earn).

⚖️ Evaluate the <b>effective yield</b>:
• <code>real APY = nominal APY × (1 - probability of asset drop)</code>

<b>Example</b>

Assume:

• Nominal APY = 12% (0.12)
• Estimated probability of a major drop (e.g., -30% or more) = 25% (0.25) — meaning a 1 in 4 chance of the asset declining in value.

Formula:

• <code>real APY = 0.12 × (1 - 0.25) = 0.12 × 0.75 = 0.09 → 9%</code>

So, although the platform promises 12% annually, the <b>risk-adjusted</b> yield may be closer to 9%.

💡 <i>Tip:</i> Don’t focus solely on the percentage — also evaluate the project’s reputation, liquidity, and the risk of funds being locked.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>3️⃣ Term Length & Liquidity</b>

⏳ <b>Short term (1–30 days):</b> more flexibility, lower risks, lower APY.
📆 <b>Long term (60–180 days):</b> more stable yield but lower liquidity.

🪙 <b>Pro tip:</b> Split your deposit (“Ladder Strategy”):
• 30% – Flexible
• 40% – 30-day Locked
• 30% – 90-day Locked

This ensures both liquidity and a steady income flow.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>4️⃣ Exchange: Reliability Above All</b>

🏦 Choose only well-established platforms:
• <b>Binance Earn</b> — largest SAFU insurance fund.
• <b>Bybit Earn</b> — high liquidity and flexible terms.
• <b>OKX Grow</b> — innovative products (Shark Fin, Dual Investment).

🔐 Check for licenses, proof-of-reserves, and user insurance coverage.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>5️⃣ Additional Factors</b>

📊 <b>Diversification:</b> don’t keep all funds in one asset or on one exchange.
🧾 <b>Taxes:</b> in most countries, APY is considered income — include this in your planning.
⚠️ <b>Slashing:</b> for staking (especially PoS), validators may be penalized for improper performance.

<b>💡 Example Portfolio Structure</b>

• 40% — Stablecoins (USDT, USDC) in Flexible products
• 30% — Top-5 coins in Locked Earn (ETH, BTC, TON)
• 20% — DeFi / Launchpool programs
• 10% — High-risk staking projects with APY > 15%

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>✅ Advantages of a Well-Chosen Asset Mix:</b>

• Optimized returns without excessive risk
• Liquidity control
• Reduced impact of market volatility
• Ability to reinvest regularly

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>⚠️ Common Mistakes:</b>

❌ Chasing the highest APY without assessing risks
❌ Using questionable DeFi projects without audits
❌ Ignoring early withdrawal restrictions

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>📘 Conclusion:</b>
📌 Earn product yields have increased recently, but so has competition between platforms.
🎯 Your choice should be based not only on APY, but also on the <b>reliability of the exchange, stability of the asset, and your liquidity strategy</b>.
