🌐 <b>Diversification: How to Allocate Your Assets</b>

<b>📋 Diversification Rules:</b>

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>1️⃣ Never keep all your assets in one coin or on a single exchange</b>

<b>2️⃣ Allocate between different types of assets</b>
• <b>Stablecoins</b> — protection from volatility
• <b>Blue-chip cryptocurrencies</b> (BTC, ETH) — stability and long-term growth
• <b>Altcoins</b> — potential for high returns, but with increased risk

<b>3️⃣ Use multiple exchanges and platforms</b>
The risks of centralized exchanges are real. Spread your funds across 3–4 trusted platforms.

<b>4️⃣ Combine different earning products</b>
• <b>Flexible</b> — liquidity, funds can be withdrawn anytime
• <b>Locked</b> — higher APY, but assets are locked for a fixed period

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>📈 OPTIMAL PORTFOLIO ALLOCATION</b>

<b>Strategies for different risk levels:</b>

<b>🟢 CONSERVATIVE PORTFOLIO</b>
Ideal for low-risk investors:

• <b>60%</b> — Stablecoins (USDT, USDC, DAI)
• <b>30%</b> — Bitcoin (BTC)
• <b>10%</b> — Ethereum (ETH)

<b>Example:</b> Portfolio $10,000
→ $6,000 USDT Flexible (Binance/Bybit) — 4–5% APY
→ $3,000 BTC Flexible — 2–3% APY
→ $1,000 ETH Locked 30 days — 4–5% APY

<b>Expected annual return:</b> 3.5–4.5%

<b>🟡 BALANCED PORTFOLIO</b>
The optimal option for most investors:

• <b>50%</b> — Stablecoins (USDT, USDC)
• <b>30%</b> — BTC + ETH (20% BTC, 10% ETH)
• <b>20%</b> — Top-30 Altcoins (SOL, BNB, XRP, ADA)

<b>Example:</b> Portfolio $10,000
→ $5,000 USDT Flexible (split across 2 exchanges) — 4–6% APY
→ $2,000 BTC Locked 60 days — 5–7% APY
→ $1,000 ETH Flexible — 5% APY
→ $2,000 Altcoins (e.g., $500 in SOL, BNB, XRP, ADA) — 6–10% APY

<b>Expected annual return:</b> 5–7%

<b>🔴 AGGRESSIVE PORTFOLIO</b>
For high-volatility strategies:

• <b>30%</b> — Stablecoins
• <b>30%</b> — BTC + ETH
• <b>40%</b> — Altcoins + DeFi + emerging projects

<b>Example:</b> Portfolio $10,000
→ $3,000 USDC Flexible — 4–5% APY
→ $3,000 BTC + ETH — ~5% APY
→ $4,000 altcoins (Top-50 + DeFi)

<b>Expected return:</b> 8–15%+

<b>🔄 PORTFOLIO REBALANCING</b>

<b>What is rebalancing?</b>
It is the process of restoring the target allocation of assets in your portfolio.

<b>Why is it important?</b>
Over time, some assets grow while others fall → the portfolio becomes unbalanced → the risk profile changes.

<b>Threshold rebalancing strategy:</b>
Rebalance whenever any asset deviates from the target allocation by <b>10–15%</b>.

<b>Example:</b>
Your portfolio: 50% USDT / 30% BTC / 20% ETH
After BTC growth: 50% USDT / 40% BTC / 10% ETH <b>→ Time to rebalance!</b> Sell part of BTC, buy ETH.
