📈 <b>Monitoring: How to Track APY Changes and Risks</b>

<b>📋 Monitoring System:</b>

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>1. Use the bot for regular checks</b>
Review APY several times per week and perform a complete portfolio audit once a month.

<b>2. Track rate changes</b>
APY may change depending on:
• Demand for the product
• Market conditions
• Exchange policies

<b>3. Reinvest when APY increases</b>
If you find a better offer, consider moving your funds (always check fees).

<b>Reinvestment rule:</b>
• If APY increases by 1–2% → consider relocating
• If APY increases by 3%+ → relocation recommended
• Always check withdrawal fees

<b>Example calculation:</b>
You have 10,000 USDT on Binance at 4% APY; Bybit offers 6.5% APY:
• Difference: 2.5% × 10,000 = +250 USDT/year
• Fee: 1 USDT
• Net profit: +249 USDT/year

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>4. Monitor risks</b>

<b>Types of risks:</b>

🚨 <b>Hacking risk</b>
• Enable 2FA (Google Authenticator, not SMS)
• Use Withdrawal Whitelist
• Activate Anti-Phishing Code
• Regularly review Login History

🔄 <b>Volatility risk</b>
• Keep 50–60% of your portfolio in stablecoins
• Stake only what you believe in long-term
• Use Flexible for short-term investments

⚠️ <b>Risk of changing conditions</b>
• Read the Terms of Service
• Diversify across exchanges
• Follow official exchange channels

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>📊 Monitoring tools:</b>

<b>CeFi (exchanges):</b>
• CoinStats — portfolio monitoring
• Delta — tracking APY changes

<b>DeFi:</b>
• DeFiLlama — APY comparison
• APY.vision — Impermanent Loss calculations

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>🚨 Red flags — act immediately:</b>

❌ Exchange delays withdrawals for >24 hours
❌ Massive user complaints on Reddit
❌ Trading volume drops by 30%+
❌ Sudden Terms update without notice

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>📋 Monitoring checklist:</b>

<b>Weekly (20 minutes):</b>
☑️ Update APY table
☑️ Compare rates across exchanges
☑️ Check Login History
☑️ Review critical news

<b>Monthly (1–2 hours):</b>
☑️ Portfolio audit
☑️ Rebalancing
☑️ Change passwords (every 3 months)
☑️ Test withdrawal with a small amount

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>💡 Tip:</b>
Create a Google Sheets table to track your portfolio and APY changes.
Regular monitoring = capital protection!

<b>⚠️ Important:</b>
Use 2FA, Withdrawal Whitelist, and a Hardware Wallet for large amounts (>$10,000).
