💰 <b>How to Earn with Referral Programs</b>

<b>📋 Step-by-Step Guide:</b>

<b>1. Register through referral links</b>
✅ Sign up on the platform using your unique referral link.
✅ Make sure all requirements are met — verification, deposit, trading if required.
✅ Save promo materials (banners, links) — this will simplify future sharing.

<b>2. Receive bonuses and understand the conditions clearly</b>
• For example: Binance — up to 100 USDT in bonuses.
• Bybit — up to 5000 USDT in trading bonuses.
• Nexo — interest bonuses on first deposits.
✅ Before accepting any offer, carefully read the terms: is a deposit required? trading? maintaining a certain volume?
⚠️ Check when the bonus is credited — instantly, after meeting conditions, or with a delay.

<b>3. Invite friends — and build passive income</b>
• Share your referral link with friends, acquaintances, on social media, or in niche communities.
• Earn a percentage from your referrals' trading fees/volume or receive fixed bonuses.
• Example: if a friend invests $1000 and earns 10% APY, you may receive 20% from their trading fees — creating a passive income source without direct risk.

<b>4. Combine strategies for maximum effect</b>
• Combine: earn-product yields + referral commissions.
• Example: you invest yourself + invite others → your income = your APY + commissions from referrals.
• Use content strategy: create educational posts or videos explaining platform benefits to attract more users.

<b>⚠️ Legal and risk considerations</b>

• Referral programs in the financial sector may fall under regulatory oversight. For example, transparency is required; misleading promises may result in sanctions.
• Ensure the exchange or platform has a strong reputation and meets security standards (KYC, licenses, reserves).
• Referral activity can be temporary — do not rely solely on high income without continued engagement.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>🧮 Example (visual illustration)</b>

You register on an exchange using your referral link.
– 10 friends signed up.
– Each deposited $5000 and started trading/investing.
– The exchange pays you 30% of their trading fees (e.g., a trading fee of 0.2% = $10 per $5000).
– So monthly fees from your friends = 10 × ($5000 × 0.2%) = $100.

* Plus: you keep your own deposit and earn APY on your personal investments.

Thus:
Your income = APY + referral commissions → passive income increases over time.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>✅ Advantages of this strategy</b>

• Additional income <b>without significant personal investments</b> (income is generated solely from referral activity).
• Passive potential: once someone registers and engages — income can flow regularly.
• Scaling effect: the more active referrals you have, the higher your earnings.
