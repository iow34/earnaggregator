🔍 <b>How to Check an Exchange’s Reliability Before Investing</b>

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>📋 Key Criteria for Exchange Reliability:</b>

<b>1. 📊 Proof-of-Reserves (PoR)</b>
• Check whether the exchange publishes reserve audits: are reports and cryptographic proofs available?
• Example: Kraken provides access to a Merkle tree, allowing users to verify whether their balance was included.
• Reserves should exceed liabilities — this is a strong indicator of safety.
⚠️ However: even PoR does not guarantee complete security — always check additional factors.

<b>2. 🔐 Account Security</b>
An exchange should provide:
• KYC/identity verification — minimum level of accountability
• 2FA (two-factor authentication) — essential
• Whitelisted withdrawal addresses — significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized withdrawals
• Anti-phishing code — protects against phishing attempts
Skipping these features is a major red flag.

<b>3. 💬 Reputation</b>
Check:
• Ratings on CoinMarketCap or similar platforms
• User reviews on forums (e.g., r/CryptoCurrency on Reddit) — users often report issues with withdrawals, support, or security
• History of hacks or incidents — any withdrawal freeze or major loss is a serious warning sign
• Exchange age — platforms operating for more than 3 years usually have more stable operations and a longer track record

<b>4. 🏦 Insurance Fund (SAFU or equivalent)</b>
• For example, Binance has the SAFU fund to protect users.
• Check whether the exchange has a reserve or insurance fund for emergencies.
• Absence of such a fund increases risk.

<b>5. 📜 Licensing & Regulation</b>
• Check whether the exchange is registered in a reputable jurisdiction and has licenses for storing/handling digital assets.
• Compliance with regulations: KYC/AML processes, regular financial reporting
• Lack of regulation does not always mean the exchange is unsafe, but it does increase risk.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>⚠️ Red Flags:</b>
• Frequent freezes or delays in withdrawals
• Lack of transparency in reserves: insufficient public data
• Poor customer support: long response times or no response at all
• Anonymous team: no public information about founders or leadership

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

<b>💡 Tip:</b>
Avoid platforms without insurance or with a history of recurring issues. Always check the exchange rating on CoinMarketCap and review authentic user feedback. Choose exchanges that provide public Proof-of-Reserves, strong account security, a clean reputation, and clear regulatory oversight.
