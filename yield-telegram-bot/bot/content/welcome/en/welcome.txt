👋 <b>Welcome to ChainAdvisor Bot!</b>

This bot aggregates publicly available Simple Earn data from major centralized exchanges.

<b>⚠️ Disclaimer:</b>
• No financial advice provided
• Information for educational purposes only
• Users make independent decisions

<b>📱 How to enable the command menu:</b>
1. Tap the menu icon (☰) at the bottom left of your keyboard
2. Select "Menu" to see all available commands

<b>🎯 Available Commands:</b>
• /main - Show main menu with product data
• /help - Detailed guide on using the bot
• /recommendations - Educational materials about products
• /exchanges - Browse products by exchange

<b>💡 Getting Started:</b>
Type /main to explore available product information.

Need help? Type /help anytime.
